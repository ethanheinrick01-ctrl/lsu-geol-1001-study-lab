Sediment on a continental slope becomes unstable and fails. The mixture of sediment and water is denser than the surrounding seawater, so gravity drives it downslope. A turbidity current can erode a submarine canyon and carry sediment into deep water. At the foot of the slope, it spreads, slows, and deposits a submarine fan. Coarser grains settle before finer grains, leaving a graded bed that becomes finer upward.
