A continental hotspot connects processes above and below the surface. In the textbook model, hot, mostly solid mantle rises toward the lithosphere. Decompression produces partial melting, while heat can also cause melting in surrounding material. Heating and thinning the lithosphere encourage broad uplift. The rising mantle is not itself a fully molten column.

Doming and stretching can organize into three rift arms. Normal faults lower blocks into basins, which collect sediment and volcanic rocks. Basaltic magma may erupt as extensive flows; heating continental crust can generate more felsic magma. In Afar, the Red Sea, Gulf of Aden, and East African Rift meet. The two seas have spreading centers, but the text does not guarantee that the African rift will split the continent completely.

Yellowstone marks the interpreted current hotspot location in the supplied model. The Snake River Plain records North America's southwestward movement over it. Large caldera-forming eruptions shaped Yellowstone's history, while retained heat drives its geothermal features. The nearby Basin and Range contains extension and fault blocks. An age-progressive track records relative movement; do not reverse plate motion and hotspot-track direction.

In one common evolutionary sequence, two rift arms open into a new ocean while the third fails to separate the continent. Rivers can follow the low failed rift and deliver sediment to a delta. Continued spreading moves the ridge away from the continental edge. The edge cools and subsides, becoming a passive margin because the active plate boundary is now offshore.

The inward bend along western equatorial Africa illustrates how rifting can influence a continental outline. Two arms helped open the South Atlantic; the failed arm remained within Africa. Use the map and the cross sections together: the map shows the branching geometry, while the sections explain thinning, new ocean formation, and later subsidence. This is an evolutionary model, not a promise that every hotspot follows every step.
