Shorten the layers
Compression shortens layered rock. When conditions favor ductile deformation, layers bend. Watch the beds and their boundaries change shape together.

Identify the folds
An upright arch with older layers in its core is an anticline. A trough with younger central layers is a syncline. A step-like bend is a monocline. Ages matter when later deformation overturns layers.

Read orientation
Limbs meet near a hinge. An axial surface connects hinges through successive layers. Tilt that surface to make an inclined fold. A plunging axis slopes into the ground, changing the map pattern. Describe geometry before inferring history.
