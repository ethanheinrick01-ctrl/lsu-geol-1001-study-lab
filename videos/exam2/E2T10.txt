Develop a fabric
Differential stress can rotate minerals and favor growth in preferred orientations. Fine cleavage in slate differs from larger aligned mica in schist. Foliation is a planar fabric, not one rock name.

Grow and separate minerals
Changing conditions drive mineral reactions and grain growth. Coarse light and dark bands characterize gneiss. Parent composition matters; every rock does not automatically follow this sequence.

Identify nonfoliated rocks
Quartz-rich sandstone can recrystallize into quartzite. Carbonate rock can become marble. Both can have interlocking crystals without foliation. Use composition as well as texture. Lack of foliation does not exclude metamorphism.
