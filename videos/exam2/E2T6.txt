Move landward
As relative sea level rises, marine environments shift over former coastal and land settings. Watch the shoreline move landward: transgression. Subsidence can contribute without a global rise in sea level.

Move seaward
Now the shoreline moves seaward. Coastal sediment can overlie former offshore deposits. Regression and progradation reflect the balance between water level, available space, and sediment supply.

Read the record
Erosion removes part of the record before deposition resumes. Textures, fossils, and structures help reconstruct the history. A porous reservoir beneath an impermeable seal can trap migrated petroleum. Reservoir rock alone does not prove a usable resource.
