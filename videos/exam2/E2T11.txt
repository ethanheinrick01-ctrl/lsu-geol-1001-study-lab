Change a solid rock
Metamorphism changes solid rock. Grains recrystallize, grow, or react to form new minerals. If rock melts, that part of its history follows an igneous pathway.

Compare pressure and temperature
Near an intrusion, heating can occur without much additional burial. Regional burial can increase pressure and temperature. A cool subducting slab reaches high pressure at lower temperature than surrounding deep crust. Read both axes.

Add fluid interaction
Active fluids move through pores and fractures, adding or removing components. Parent composition controls which minerals can form. Minerals and fabrics constrain the history, but grade and tectonic setting require several pieces of evidence.
