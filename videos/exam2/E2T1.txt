From slope to channel
Steep slopes produce angular rock fragments. Flowing water carries sediment into channels, while floods spread finer material across a floodplain. Watch the grains move; the setting is defined by its transport and deposition, not just its location.

Build the river mouth
When the river enters standing water, it slows. Coarser sediment accumulates near the mouth, building a delta. Finer particles can remain suspended and travel farther. A delta and a floodplain are different parts of the river system.

Read the coast and ocean
Waves work the beach. A barrier shelters a lagoon. Offshore, fine material settles, and sediment-laden turbidity currents can move downslope. Compare grain size, structures, and biological remains before choosing an ancient environment.
