Ancient basement. Metamorphic and plutonic basement formed about 1.7 billion years ago.

Precambrian cover. Sedimentary and volcanic cover accumulated about 1.1 billion years ago.

Tilt and erode. Tilting and erosion removed rock and left an angular unconformity.

Paleozoic deposition. Sandstone, shale and limestone accumulated above the eroded surface.

Canyon incision. Much later, the river cut through the old rocks, exposing their long history.

Two different ages. Old canyon-wall rocks do not give the age of canyon cutting.
