Parent isotopes. Begin with parent atoms in an ideal closed system.

First half-life. The parent fraction falls from 100% to 50% during this interval.

Second half-life. Half of the remaining parents decay; the endpoint is 25% parent and 75% daughter.

Third half-life. The parent fraction falls from 25% to 12.5%; equal time removes a smaller amount.

Read the clock. Ratios constrain age only when the dating assumptions are evaluated.
