William Smith recognized recurring fossils in the same rock units while surveying, even where faults and folds interrupted their exposure. Systematic faunal succession made correlation and geological mapping possible. Stratigraphy combines rock types, fossil evidence and environments; a convincing correlation explains both the matches and the differences.
