Stress is force per area. Strain is a change in shape or volume. With elastic strain, removing the load restores the sample. Permanent deformation occurs by fracture or ductile change.

On the course graph, depth increases downward and strength increases rightward. In the brittle upper crust, increasing confinement makes fractures harder to develop. The curve approaches a maximum.

Deeper, higher temperature promotes solid-state ductile flow. Watch the solid rock bend. The curve turns toward lower strength. The brittle–ductile transition is gradual; its depth depends on conditions, not one universal number.
