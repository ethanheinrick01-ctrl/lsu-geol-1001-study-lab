# Material artwork

The prompt requested eight equal flat material swatches in a 4 × 2 atlas, without labels or perspective: basalt, stratified sandstone, upper mantle, deeper mantle, ocean bathymetry, land surface, volcanic deposits, and molten lava. It specified the reference artwork's hand-painted rock grain, subtle natural variation, and colors. The output is texture artwork; it does not determine geological motion or geometry.

The renderer maps these swatches onto explicitly authored geometry. Original course images remain on the Learn pages with their existing attribution. No image-generation or other API runs when the student opens or uses the Lab.
