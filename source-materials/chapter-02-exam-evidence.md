## Trap map

- Calling a cause an observation.
- Giving older/younger order without naming the principle.
- Reversing cross-cutting or inclusions.
- Confusing elevation with relief or slope; omitting units.
- Treating close contours as high elevation rather than steep slope.
- Confusing a cross section, block diagram, and stratigraphic section.
- Memorizing a crater picture instead of testing model-specific predictions.
- Treating a circular pattern or bent layers as proof of one cause.
